## Local choices

- Both sources inform `app/mail/customer_voice.md`, the single runtime profile. Domain-specific guidance and examples are written for Austrian Power.
- Our explicit house rules override upstream allowances for dashes or alternative signatures. Natural contractions, greetings and genuine next steps remain welcome.
- No AI-detector bypass claim or score is adopted. No fake opinions, personal anecdotes, deliberate mistakes or invented shipment facts are added.
- The profile guides the existing composer and repair attempt. It does not add another model pass, execute upstream scripts or fetch anything in production.
- Existing evidence, price, coverage, cutoff, reference and send controls remain authoritative.
